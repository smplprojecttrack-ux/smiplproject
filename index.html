Calls to make (0)

Timeline period
Route stop: vehicle stopped outside plant, queue and mine for more than the route limit. Call the driver or supervisor. Loading delay: vehicle inside a mine (Loading geofence) longer than the loading limit. Call that mine's loading manager (the in-charge saved on the geofence in Places and people). Unloading delay: inside the unloading point or its queue longer than the unloading limit. After a call, the case stays "Called" until the follow-up time passes; if the vehicle is still standing it comes back as "Follow-up due". Limits are in Settings.

Map and direction

Draw a circle, rectangle or polygon with the tools on the left of the map to create a geofence. Use the edit (pencil) tool to change an existing shape.
